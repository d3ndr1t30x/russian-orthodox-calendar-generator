# Russian Orthodox Calendar Generator 1.3.0

This release adds source-derived liturgical service-rank publishing to the bilingual A4 calendar.

## Included

- Great Feast, Vigil, Polyeleos, Doxology, Six Stichera and No-Sign classifications.
- Original Holy Trinity Typikon sign preservation and bilingual rank terminology.
- Distinct embedded rank icons and a compact English/Russian PDF legend.
- Independent rendering of service rank, fasting permissions and Australian civil holidays.
- Rank provenance and clearly marked overrides in the calendar editor.
- A4 landscape monthly design, bilingual source content and embedded Cyrillic fonts.
- Standalone Windows x64 distribution requiring neither Python nor the legacy .NET application.

## Verification

- 42 automated tests passed.
- The packaged EXE launched successfully.
- The packaged EXE generated and validated 12-page English and Russian A4 landscape PDFs.
- Compiled English and Russian pages were raster-rendered and visually inspected for clipping, overlap, icon placement and Cyrillic rendering.

Calendar content should be verified against the current official liturgical calendar used by the relevant parish or diocese. Australian public holidays are civil information.
